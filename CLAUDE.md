# PRIME DIRECTIVES — Meta/Facebook Ad Compliance Guardrails

These rules apply to ALL ad creative work in this project (the "TRUE FALSE" color-block
identification campaign and any derivatives). Treat them as non-negotiable constraints,
not suggestions.

## 1. Imagery must never imply the viewer's condition

Never pair first-person confession copy with imagery that implies the viewer's
condition — no clutched chests, no hospital beds, no "before" bodies. First-person
copy with second-person imagery re-triggers Meta's personal-attributes filter.
Use the narrator's face — the YouTube channel data proves his face already works.

## 2. The landing page must stay in first person

Meta reviews the destination, not just the ad. A confession-style ad pointing to a
"YOUR arteries are clogged" page gets the entire ad set flagged. Keep landing-page
copy in the same first-person voice as the ad.

## 3. No outcomes, timelines, or "reverse/cure/unclog" in the ad layer

Save protocol and outcome language for the video content, where it lives natively.
The ad's only job is identification: "this is me" → click.

## 4. Quarantine the powerful-but-fragile blocks in separate campaigns

The three highest-risk / highest-power confession blocks must each launch in their
own campaign, so a single rejection cannot take down healthy ad sets with it:

- "I got my test results, and I've been carrying that number around ever since."
  (post-diagnosis limbo)
- "There are things going on with my body I haven't even told my wife."
  (private symptoms / ED-vascular doorway)
- "Every chest twinge, I quietly wonder: is this it?"
  (hypervigilance after a scare)
