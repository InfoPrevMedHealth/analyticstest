# Pain Point #21 — "The Night Researcher" (Intuition Build) — July 2026

A speculative build with no test data behind it, run on intuition from the
Frank profile: nearly half of health searches are made **for someone else**,
and none of the twenty tested pain points speaks in that voice. This
document adds the researching spouse / adult child as a new pain point with
its own avatar, six concepts, copy-paste image prompts, and the quarantine
structure it requires.

## The candidate confession

> "I read about heart disease late at night. Not for me — for him."

## Diane — the night researcher (avatar, inferred from the Frank profile)

Diane is Frank's counterpart: the person in the household who does the
worrying out loud to a search bar and nowhere else.

- Spouse (or adult child) of a Frank — midlife-to-older, the household's
  health quarterback: books the appointments, notices the breathlessness on
  the stairs, remembers his father's age when it happened.
- Researches at night, phone-lit, after he's asleep. Her search history is
  a love letter written in fear: symptoms, tests, "how do I get my husband
  to see a doctor."
- Her constraint is his pride. She can't push too hard or he digs in — so
  she gathers evidence quietly and forwards videos casually. "Worth ten
  minutes" is how love talks in this house.
- The adult-child variant: the role reversal moment — worrying about Dad
  the way Dad used to worry about them, often triggered by grandfather's
  history repeating.
- What she wants from a click: not a diagnosis — a credible, calm,
  doctor-shaped voice she can hand to him without it feeling like an
  accusation.

## Compliance architecture (read before trafficking)

1. **Structurally compliant by design.** The viewer is the researcher; the
   confession describes the *viewer's own behavior* (researching), never
   the viewer's condition. The at-risk person is third person ("him,"
   "Dad") — outside the personal-attributes trap entirely.
2. **Fragile anyway — quarantine it.** The secrecy theme ("he has no
   idea") is the mirror image of the quarantined "haven't told my wife"
   block. Same treatment: **own campaign, small budget, no shared fate
   with healthy ad sets.**
3. **No people in imagery.** Not the loved one (implies his condition),
   and not a worried woman (casts the viewer). Objects only: the lit
   phone, the tabs, the dark kitchen. The one exception is the narrator's
   real face, as ever.
4. **⚠ Landing-page bridge is the unresolved item.** Prime Directive #2
   keeps ad and destination in one first-person voice — but the existing
   pages speak as the at-risk man, and a spouse-voiced ad clicking into
   "my arteries" copy breaks continuity. Recommended bridge, in the
   narrator's own first person (compliant, and true to channel data):
   *"About half the people who watch my videos aren't watching for
   themselves."* Add that line (or a variant page led by it) before this
   campaign traffics. **Do not launch #21 pointing at an unmodified
   Frank-voice page.**

## Test plan for #21

- **Own campaign, quarantine rules** (see Compliance architecture): small
  budget, no shared fate with the four proven pain points.
- Launch order: Concept 1 (block control) + Concept 3 (1:30 A.M. Search)
  first — the control gives comparability to the original twenty, the
  search concept is the intuition bet. Add 2/5/6 as budget allows;
  Concept 4 last (the secrecy line is the most fragile element in the
  whole batch).
- Same success bar as everything else: beat the block control at ≥300
  impressions; kill anything under 3% CTR at that volume.
- **Blocker before launch:** the landing-page bridge (Compliance item 4).
  One narrator-voice line acknowledging the on-behalf viewer is the
  minimum viable fix.
- What a win here means: if #21 clears the bar, the on-behalf audience is
  real and it unlocks a "for her" variant, a sibling/parent variant, and a
  dedicated forwardable-page strategy. If it flops at the block-control
  level, the voice doesn't identify on Facebook and the subsegment gets
  reached organically instead (they already forward the videos).
